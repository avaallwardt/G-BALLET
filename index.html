What is an arabesque?

An arabesque is one of the most fundamental poses in ballet. The dancer balances on one straight leg while the other leg extends straight behind the body, with the arms reaching outward and the chest lifted. It looks simple, but every part of the body has a precise position. Small differences in angles and alignment differentiate good and excellent arabesque technique.

How it works

  1. 1Extract landmarksMediaPipe finds 33 body points in the photo: shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, ankles, heels, and toes.
  2. 2Compute the geometryA geometric framework I designed turns those points into angles, lines, and distances, like knee angle, leg height, and how square the hips and shoulders are.
  3. 3Criteria scoresEach measurement is checked against the technique criteria for an arabesque and marked as met, a moderate issue, or a major issue.
  4. 4FeedbackEvery missed criteria gets a correction, shown with the measurement and the target.
